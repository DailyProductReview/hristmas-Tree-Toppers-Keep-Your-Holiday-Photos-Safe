Why Holiday Photo Backup Is Harder Than It Should Be

Most people have tried at least one backup method. You might have an external hard drive, a USB stick, or a free cloud account. These solutions seem fine until you actually need them. External drives fail, get lost, or sit unplugged for months. Free cloud services fill up after a few dozen photos. Emailing files to yourself is messy and compresses images. And relying on your phone storage is risky. One drop, one theft, one ransomware attack, and everything is gone.

The real problem is that all these methods require you to remember to back up. During the holidays, you are busy. You are decorating the tree, placing the topper, hosting family, and opening gifts. The last thing on your mind is manually transferring files. That is why a truly effective solution must be automatic and constant. It should work in the background without you lifting a finger.
